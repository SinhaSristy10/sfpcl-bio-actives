01 / Pomegranate

PuniCore™

Pomegranate Peel Extract

A polyphenol-rich extract from export-grade Indian pomegranate, naturally rich in punicalagin. Developed to support antioxidant functionality and freshness in food and beverage formulations.

Grades

SF-P 40SF-EP30SF-EP20SF-EP10SF-AP20SF-AP10

Applications & product details

  • SF-P 40 is our highest grade, standardised up to 40% punicalagin.
  • Applications include oils and fats, processed foods, beverages and nutraceutical formulations.
  • Helps protect flavour and colour by delaying oxidation.
  • Water soluble, with polyphenols up to 80% depending on grade.
  • Contact our technical team to select the appropriate grade.

02 / Citrus

Citrus Fibre

Texture, moisture retention & mouthfeel

Extracted from Indian citrus peel. A multifunctional ingredient for managing water, improving texture and supporting formulation stability.

Grades

SLFSOFSPFSLDFSLDF-100 (Depectinized)

Applications & product details

  • Applications include dairy, bakery, sauces, beverages and frozen foods.
  • Supports moisture retention, stability and mouthfeel.
  • Can support reformulation of starch, gum and fat systems, depending on the application.
